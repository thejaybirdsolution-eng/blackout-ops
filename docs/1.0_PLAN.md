## 3. Mode 1.0 Promises (1-Hour & 10-Hour Player Experience)

- **Zombies:** Within the first hour, a player flips power cleanly without guessing what is next, earns Pack-a-Punch upgrades through clear per-map signposting, and fights escalating special rounds that showcase 70 zombies and 15 bosses. After 10 hours, chasing map challenges, per-map high-round leaderboards, and co-op revive/support synergy turns every arena into a replayable high-score chase.
- **Tower Defense:** Within the first hour, every intermission gives tactical agency—knowing what wave archetype arrives next and placing Sentry + Guard Dog + Marine crossfires that unlock tangible synergy buffs. After 10 hours, players push all 3 Castle keeps across curated `ModeModifiers` difficulty tiers to set top wave records.
- **Multiplayer:** Within the first hour, players feel crisp hitreg, fair spawns, human-paced bots across all 6 classic arenas and 5 core rulesets, and an authentic lobby -> match -> scoreboard -> XP -> Next Map Vote loop. After 10 hours, custom preset rules and weapon mastery grind cross-unlock camos usable in every other mode.
- **Survival (Exclusion Zone):** Within the first hour, the opening **Prepare -> Expedition -> Haul -> Invest -> Defend** cycle hooks immediately via Day-1 setup, Safe Room shelter, and dawn upkeep. After 10 hours, liberating outposts, assembling vehicles, recruiting settlers, and claiming light pushes cleanly toward confronting the Warden and choosing one of the Lantern's three endings.
- **Rogue Lite (Blackout Vaults):** Within the first hour, elemental Primer -> Detonator reactions, Boons, and mystery rooms produce distinct build synergies by Floor 5 and an instructive debrief after every run. After 10 hours, unlocking all 12 operatives, 18 curios, 288 Haven cosmetics, Deep Vaults (26–50+), and 2–4p online delves turns the Vaults into an endless mastery loop.
- **Open World RPG (Operation Meridian):** Within the first hour, Haven Basin sets the 3-path quest rule (combat/tech/social), deterministic skill checks, stealth, and skyline navigation. After 10 hours, players resolve multi-region faction storylines across all 6 core zones with recruited combat companions, watch faction territory and NPC schedules react, unlock all elemental abilities, and restart in New Game+.
- **Minigames:** Within the first hour, 40+ crisp thumbnailed arcade/target/speed/boss challenges deliver instant restart flow, escalating streak combos, and a seeded Daily Course. After 10 hours, chasing Platinum stars, PB Ghost split deltas, and online Versus lobbies keeps arcade competition sharp.

## 5. Capstone Selection — One-Page Pitches

### Option A: Operation Blackout (10-Mission Cross-Mode Story Campaign) — **SELECTED**
- **Pitch:** A linear 10-chapter military thriller (`CampaignData.js`, `CampaignManager.js`, `CampaignUI.js`) starring Commander Kessler, Engineer Sola, Lt. Vance, and Operator Mercer. Rather than building one throwaway campaign mode, every mission launches directly into one of BLACKOUT OPS's existing 7 modes (`range_day` qualification -> `killhouse` TDM -> `der_bunker` outbreak -> `td_castle_1` defense -> `exclusion_zone` crash -> `rpg` vault breach -> `owrpg` Meridian signal -> `bandit_town` convoy pursuit -> `mp_nuketown` relay recapture -> `nuketown_zombies` final boss containment).
- **System Reuse:** **~95%** — reuses existing maps, AI, Gunsmith classes, HUD objective pill, `BO.Menus`, `BO.Sound`, and `BO.Save.data.profile`. Solves new-player discoverability by turning the entire game into a cohesive guided tour.

### Option B: Seasonal Rotating Modifier Track (50 Free Tiers)
- **Pitch:** A offline-deterministic 50-tier season pass backed by `ModeModifiersCatalog.js` weekly playlists.
- **Why Runner-Up:** Great retention hook later, but adds horizontal grind over existing modes without uniting the disjoint modes under a single narrative climax for a brand-new player.

### Option C: Cross-Mode Arsenal Mastery Gallery
- **Pitch:** Weapon-by-weapon 70-gun mastery tracker across all modes with final class diamond camos.
- **Why Runner-Up:** Partially folded directly into Section 3 (**Unified Armory**), where all 70 weapons and camo paths are already surfaced cleanly without needing to stand alone as the Section 5 capstone.

## 6. Online Scope Decision (`ADR-017` Summary)
- **Shipped in 1.0:** Rogue Lite Co-op (2–4 players, host-authoritative enemies/doors, per-player loot & Profile XP) + Minigames Same-Room Versus & Async Seeded Leaderboards.
- **Deferred Post-1.0:** Full-world co-op for Exclusion Zone Survival and Operation Meridian OWRPG (25+ coupled `GM*`/`Zone*` client state machines and branching deterministic quest/interior state graphs require dedicated host-replicated world architecture to avoid desyncs).
